How to Sign In to Your Peacock TV Account
-----------------------------------------

Open the Peacock TV website or launch the Peacock TV app → Click **Sign In** → Enter your registered email address → Type your account password → Select **Sign In** → Complete any verification step if prompted → Access your Peacock TV account and begin streaming your favorite content.

Common Sign-In Problems
-----------------------

* Incorrect email address or password.
* Forgotten account password.
* Slow or unstable internet connection.
* Outdated Peacock TV application.
* Browser cache or app cache issues.
* Temporary Peacock TV server outage.
* Subscription or billing problems.
* Device compatibility issues.
* VPN or proxy connection enabled.

How to Fix Peacock TV Sign-In Issues
------------------------------------

Verify Your Login Credentials
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Check that your email address and password are entered correctly. Make sure there are no typing errors.

Reset Your Password
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

If you have forgotten your password, use the **Forgot Password** option to create a new secure password.

Check Your Internet Connection
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Ensure your internet connection is stable before attempting to sign in.

Update the Peacock TV App
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Install the latest version of the Peacock TV application to improve compatibility and performance.

Clear Cache and Cookies
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Clear your browser cache or the Peacock TV app cache if your device supports this option.

Restart Your Device
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Restart your Smart TV, streaming device, smartphone, tablet, or computer before trying again.

Disable VPN or Proxy
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Turn off any VPN or proxy service that could interfere with account authentication.

Reinstall the Peacock TV App
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

If sign-in problems continue, uninstall and reinstall the Peacock TV app before logging in again.
